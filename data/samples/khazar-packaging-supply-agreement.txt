PACKAGING SUPPLY AND DESIGN SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 1 November 2026 between:

(1) Khazar Packaging Industries LLC, a company registered in the Republic of Azerbaijan, whose registered office is at 14 Babek Avenue, Baku (the "Supplier"); and

(2) Araz Retail Group LLC, a company registered in the Republic of Azerbaijan, whose registered office is at 28 May Street, Baku (the "Company").

BACKGROUND

(A) The Supplier manufactures printed packaging and provides packaging design services.
(B) The Company wishes to purchase packaging and design services from the Supplier for its private-label product range, and the Supplier is willing to supply them on the terms of this Agreement.

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement the following words have the following meanings. "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in the Republic of Azerbaijan. "Deliverables" means all artwork, designs, dielines, mock-ups, specifications and other materials created by the Supplier for the Company under this Agreement. "Fees" means the charges payable by the Company for the Goods and Services as set out in Schedule 2. "Goods" means the printed packaging described in Schedule 1. "Order" means a written purchase order issued by the Company. "Services" means the packaging design services described in Schedule 1.

1.2 Clause headings are for convenience only and do not affect the interpretation of this Agreement. References to clauses and Schedules are to the clauses of and Schedules to this Agreement. Words in the singular include the plural and the other way round. A reference to writing includes email.

2. SUPPLY OF GOODS AND SERVICES

2.1 The Supplier shall supply the Goods and perform the Services in accordance with each Order, the specifications in Schedule 1 and all laws that apply to food-contact packaging in the Republic of Azerbaijan.

2.2 The Company is not obliged to place any minimum number of Orders or to purchase any minimum volume of Goods. No Order is binding on the Supplier until the Supplier has confirmed it in writing, which it shall do within two (2) Business Days of receipt.

3. ORDERS, FORECASTS AND DELIVERY

3.1 The Company shall provide the Supplier with a non-binding rolling forecast of its expected requirements for the following three (3) months on or before the first Business Day of each month.

3.2 The Supplier shall deliver the Goods to the Company's distribution centre in Sumgayit on the delivery date stated in the Order. Delivery is complete when the Goods have been unloaded and a delivery note has been signed by the Company's receiving staff. Risk in the Goods passes to the Company on completion of delivery, and title passes on payment.

3.3 If the Supplier expects any delay, it shall notify the Company as soon as it becomes aware of the delay and shall state the cause and the revised delivery date.

4. QUALITY, INSPECTION AND REJECTION

4.1 The Supplier warrants that the Goods shall conform to the agreed specifications and approved print proofs, shall be free from defects in materials and workmanship, and shall be fit for packing food products.

4.2 The Company may inspect the Goods within ten (10) Business Days of delivery and may reject any Goods that do not conform to clause 4.1. The Supplier shall, at the Company's choice, replace rejected Goods within seven (7) Business Days or refund the Fees paid for them.

5. FEES, INVOICING AND PAYMENT

5.1 The Fees are fixed for the first twelve (12) months of this Agreement. After that the Supplier may propose a revised price list once in each calendar year by giving sixty (60) days' written notice, and the revised prices apply only if the Company accepts them in writing.

5.2 The Supplier shall invoice the Company on or after completion of delivery of each Order. Each invoice shall quote the Order number and shall be sent to the Company's accounts payable department by email.

5.3 The Company shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt. If the Company disputes an invoice in good faith, it shall notify the Supplier within ten (10) Business Days of receipt, pay the undisputed part, and the parties shall work to resolve the dispute promptly.

5.4 If the Company fails to pay an undisputed amount by its due date, the Supplier may charge a penalty of 0.1% of the overdue amount for each day of delay, up to a maximum of ten percent (10%) of the overdue amount.

5.5 All Fees are stated exclusive of value added tax, which the Company shall pay at the rate required by law on receipt of a valid tax invoice.

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1 All Deliverables, and all intellectual property rights in them, shall be owned by the Company upon payment of the Fees for the Order to which they relate. The Supplier shall sign any document reasonably needed to record that ownership.

6.2 The Supplier keeps ownership of its pre-existing tools, templates and manufacturing know-how, and grants the Company a perpetual, royalty-free licence to use them to the extent they are embedded in the Deliverables.

6.3 The Supplier warrants that the Deliverables shall not infringe the intellectual property rights of any third party.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each party shall keep the other party's confidential information confidential during this Agreement and for three (3) years after its termination, and shall use it only to perform this Agreement.

7.2 Clause 7.1 does not apply to information that is or becomes public other than through a breach of this Agreement, that the receiving party already lawfully held, or that the receiving party is required by law to disclose.

8. DATA PROTECTION

8.1 Where the Supplier handles personal data on behalf of the Company, including consumer names and addresses used for promotional mailings, it shall do so only on the Company's written instructions and shall keep that data secure.

8.2 The Supplier shall notify the Company of any personal data breach within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of it and shall give the Company the information it reasonably needs to meet its own legal obligations.

9. SUBCONTRACTING AND PERSONNEL

9.1 The Supplier shall not subcontract any part of the Services or the manufacture of the Goods without the Company's prior written consent. The Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of any approved subcontractor.

9.2 The Supplier shall ensure that its personnel attending the Company's premises comply with the Company's site rules and health and safety policies as notified to the Supplier.

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits either party's liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for any other liability that cannot be limited by law.

10.2 Subject to clause 10.1, each party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the Fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. This cap applies equally to both parties.

10.3 Subject to clause 10.1, neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential losses, including loss of profit.

11. INSURANCE

11.1 The Supplier shall maintain product liability insurance and public liability insurance with a reputable insurer throughout this Agreement and shall provide evidence of cover on request.

12. TERM

12.1 This Agreement starts on 1 November 2026 and continues until 31 October 2028 (the "Initial Term").

12.2 At the end of the Initial Term, and at the end of each renewal period, this Agreement shall renew automatically for a further period of twelve (12) months unless either party has given notice of non-renewal in accordance with clause 16.

13. TERMINATION

13.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving thirty (30) days' written notice to the other party.

13.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within thirty (30) days of being asked to do so, or becomes insolvent.

13.3 On termination the Supplier shall complete and deliver all Orders confirmed before the termination date, and the Company shall pay for them in accordance with clause 5.

14. FORCE MAJEURE

14.1 Neither party is in breach of this Agreement or liable for delay in performing its obligations if the delay results from events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, war, and acts of government. The affected party shall notify the other party promptly and shall use reasonable efforts to limit the effect of the event.

14.2 If a force majeure event continues for more than sixty (60) days, either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice without liability.

15. ANTI-BRIBERY AND COMPLIANCE

15.1 Each party shall comply with all anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws that apply to it and shall not offer, give or accept any improper payment or advantage in connection with this Agreement.

15.2 The Supplier shall keep accurate records of the Goods supplied and the Services performed and shall allow the Company to inspect them on reasonable notice during business hours.

16. NOTICES AND ADMINISTRATIVE MATTERS

16.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing, in Azerbaijani or English, and shall be delivered by hand, by registered post or by email to the address of the receiving party set out at the start of this Agreement or to any other address that party has notified in writing. A notice delivered by hand is received when it is left at the address. A notice sent by registered post is received on the third Business Day after posting. A notice sent by email is received at the time of transmission, or on the next Business Day if it is sent after 18:00 or on a day that is not a Business Day, and for the purposes of clause 12.2 a notice of non-renewal is valid only if it is received by the other party not later than one hundred and eighty (180) days before the end of the Initial Term or of the renewal period then running. Routine operational communications, including Orders, forecasts and delivery schedules, may be exchanged by email between the parties' nominated contacts without following this clause.

16.2 Each party shall nominate an account manager who is the first point of contact for the other party and shall notify the other party promptly of any change.

17. GENERAL

17.1 This Agreement and its Schedules are the entire agreement between the parties about their subject matter and replace all earlier discussions and understandings.

17.2 No change to this Agreement is effective unless it is in writing and signed by both parties.

17.3 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

17.4 If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

17.5 A failure or delay by a party to exercise a right under this Agreement is not a waiver of that right.

18. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

18.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Azerbaijan.

18.2 The parties shall first try to resolve any dispute through good-faith discussion between senior managers for a period of thirty (30) days. If the dispute is not resolved in that period, it shall be resolved by the courts of Baku.
